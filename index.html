How ranking works

Every item on this wall is in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. The Threat Score is a transparent prioritization aid based on KEV status, NVD CVSS score, how recently CISA added the CVE, the federal remediation due date, and whether CISA reports known ransomware campaign use.

This dashboard is for vulnerability research and prioritization. CISA and NVD remain the authoritative sources.

Data: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities + NIST National Vulnerability Database Open source on GitHub
